About Us

Who We Are

IEEE at UCSC connects students with IEEE, the world’s largest technical professional organization. You don’t need to be an engineering major. Anyone interested in technology is welcome.

Gain Hands-On Experience

Join project teams to design, prototype, and build hardware and software from scratch. You’ll solve real problems for the local community and gain experience to show in interviews and on your resume.

Connect With Industry

Meet engineers, researchers, and recruiters at our professional events. Learn about hiring paths, technical standards, and emerging work in computing and electronics.

Networking and More

IEEE membership links you to a global network with over 2,000 conferences a year, international research, technical publications, and regional events at nearby campuses like Stanford.
